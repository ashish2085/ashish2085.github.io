About

Pipelines, models & AI — end‑to‑end.

I'm a Senior AI Data Engineer with 7+ years architecting cloud-native solutions on GCP and Snowflake — across fintech, energy, mortgage tech and consulting. I started at TCS and most recently work at Shoolin Business Consulting, building Salesforce Agentforce and EinsteinLLM frameworks.

My work spans the modern data stack — ETL/ELT with Airflow and dbt, ingestion with Airbyte and Kafka, streaming with Spark and PySpark, and shipping it all on Cloud Run, Cloud Functions and BigQuery with Docker, Terraform and GitHub Actions.

The fun part is the GenAI layer on top — RAG with LangChain and Pinecone, agent frameworks via Agentforce and EinsteinLLM, and Streamlit apps powered by Gemini and OpenAI. I like bridging the gap between complex backend engineering and user-facing insight.
